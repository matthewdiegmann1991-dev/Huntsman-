- `people-discover` no longer reports a search engine's near-matches as leads. Engines relax a quoted name ("Kylie Trong" returned 79 pages for "Kylie Luong", "Kylie Nguyen" and generic `kylie` repositories, none naming the subject). A result is now kept only when every token of the name is a whole word of its title or address, or the address runs the tokens together. Each facet reports the dropped results as `discarded_name_mismatch`. Search hits carry their anchor text internally for this; serialised search reports are unchanged.
