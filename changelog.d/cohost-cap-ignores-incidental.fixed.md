- The co-host cap counts only names a source linked to the address at or above the floor a recursive scan follows: a page domain that merely co-occurred with the address in a urlscan.io scan (an incidental pivot, below the floor) did not resolve to it, is not a co-host, and can no longer tip a dedicated address over the cap. Live, `ip 8.8.8.8` had counted 87 such pages among its 1,684 "co-hosts".
