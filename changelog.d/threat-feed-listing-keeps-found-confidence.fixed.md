- A threat-feed listing no longer raises a found name's confidence. The `ip`, `domain` and `netblock` lookups check every name they found against the feeds; a listing used to join the report at the listing's own 0.90 and the merge kept the maximum, so a co-hosted name or a page that merely involved the subject ranked beside the subject itself the moment a feed named it (live: `wheregoes.com`, tied to `example.com` only by a urlscan.io scan, reached 0.90 on a PhishTank listing of one URL on it). The listing now adds its evidence and `malicious` tag and keeps the confidence the finder gave the name; the subject itself keeps its own.
