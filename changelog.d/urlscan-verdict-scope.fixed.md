- A urlscan.io verdict judges the scanned page, so the leg now counts a malicious verdict against the indicator only when the page was on it (its domain or a name under it; for an address, served from it): `malicious_scans` and `max_verdict_score` cover those pages and alone tag the indicator `malicious` (and alone make a `MALICIOUS_*` element in the SpiderFoot-shaped export). A malicious verdict on another page that merely involved the indicator (a phishing page that loaded a resource from it, or redirected through it) is kept apart in `involved_malicious_scans` and `max_involved_verdict_score`, with `scans_on_indicator` and `scans_involving` counting both kinds.
