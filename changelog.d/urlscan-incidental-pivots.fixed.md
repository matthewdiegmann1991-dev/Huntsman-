- The urlscan.io leg now tells a scan of a page on the indicator (the page's domain is the indicator or a name under it; for an address, the page was served from it) from a scan of another page that merely involved it (loaded a resource from it, or passed through it). The first names the indicator's own pages and the address and network serving them, linked pivots as before; the second's domain, address and network, and a site that only redirected into the indicator, are now incidental: kept with their evidence, tagged `incidental`, at 0.40, below the floor a recursive scan follows. Live, `domain example.com` had emitted 95 such page domains at 0.60, every one a pivot candidate. The `incidental` reading is a general flag on indicator-source pivots (`Finding::incidental_pivot`), so any source can mark co-occurrence as such.
