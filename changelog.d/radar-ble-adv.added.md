- `radar ble-adv [--address ADDR] [--raw] [--json] HEX...` decodes, offline, the bytes of a Bluetooth LE advertisement and scan response a scanner app showed, as whole payloads or `TYPE:DATA` fragments: flags, service UUIDs (named where well known), the local name (a label the owner typed, never an identity), TX power, the appearance category, service data and manufacturer-specific data with its company named from the embedded Bluetooth SIG list. Payloads are reported by length unless `--raw` asks for the bytes; `--address` reads the device address as a Bluetooth LE address and what that means for recognising the device later. From a pasted record of a Samsung phone whose address rotated.
