- The `people` lookup (and every scan, API and MCP lookup that runs it) matches the name against
  the AEC Transparency Register's annual returns: the archive the AEC publishes whole (donations
  made and received, receipts, debts, discretionary benefits, capital contributions, and the
  party, associated-entity, donor, third-party, significant third-party and member returns) is
  kept on the device beside the sanctions lists, refreshed weekly, and read offline; every row
  whose name columns carry every word of the name is a Document lead keyed by its table,
  financial year and names, the whole row (the other party, amount, date, ABN and ACN where the
  return carries them) kept verbatim as evidence. A table the archive no longer holds is reported
  as drift, never as a clean zero.
